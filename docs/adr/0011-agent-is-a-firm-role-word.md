# "Agent" is a firm role, a code and screen word

D-5 names the roles of a firm `owner`, `lead` and `agent`, and says **firm** and **agent** are the words in code and on screens. This reverses the earlier glossary rule that listed *agent* among the words to avoid for an advisor.

The two uses do not overlap. **Advisor** stays the platform role in `user_roles` (the person who works intakes on Nevermore). **Agent** is a person's role inside one firm (`firm_memberships.role`): in Edgar Allan Advisory today's advisors are its agents (D-15). Code and screens that mean the platform role say advisor; code and screens that mean the firm role say agent.

## Considered Options

- **Keep "agent" on the avoid list and call the role something else.** Rejected: D-5 fixed the three role names, and the firm screens built on them use those words.
- **Rename the platform advisor role.** Rejected: it is in every policy and function of the intake side, and nothing is gained by touching it.

## Consequences

- The Advisor entry in `GLOSSARY.md` avoids "agent" except as the firm role.
- Using "agent" for the platform role, or "advisor" for the firm role, is a wording bug.
